## Disposition

The tied architecture is capacity-qualified, but tying Event identity is **insufficient** to produce held-out consequence or a readable relation under this exact benchmark and training signal. The result is the predeclared **T1** case and all sixteen fitted trajectories read **M4**:

```text

B  ROLE CONSEQUENCE         no,   0/16 reach t_gen

D  REPRESENTATION READABLE  no,   0/16 reach t_repr, t_repr_global or t_repr_exact
E  LOAD-BEARING LOCATION    not localizable; no generalizing behavior emerged
```

Both matched arms fit TRAIN on 8/8 seeds by update 8192 and on 8/8 seeds by the first scheduled exact-fit checkpoint at update 64. Their final median ROLE_TEST, NOVEL_TEST, probe ROLE and probe NOVEL accuracies are all `0.0`. No delayed-generalization flag fires. Input-swap invariance is bit-exact at every behavioral checkpoint in every run.

The principal finite conclusion is therefore:

> On this frozen 48/96/24 benchmark, with the matched `014.03a` optimizer settings and 65,536-update horizon, forcing each Event to use one shared trainable identity across query and candidate roles does not by itself turn answer memorization into role-neutral consequence or unseen-block relation learning.

This is a sufficiency test relative to one untied predecessor. It does not establish that tying is unnecessary in every architecture, that another tied architecture cannot grok this benchmark, or that the untied seam identified after `014.03a` was scientifically irrelevant.
