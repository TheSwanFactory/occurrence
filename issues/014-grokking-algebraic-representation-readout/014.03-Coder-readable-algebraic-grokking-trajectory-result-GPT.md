## Cause, isolated

`probe_instrument_diagnostic.json` records the isolation, run after the gate
verdict was committed and changing nothing about it.

014.02 section 4 freezes the probe optimizer as `Adam` with `weight decay 1e-3`.
In PyTorch that phrase has two implementations: a coupled L2 term added to the
gradient inside `torch.optim.Adam`, and decoupled weight decay as in
`torch.optim.AdamW`. Section 5 names `AdamW` **explicitly** for the main-model
regime grid, so the literal reading of section 4 is the coupled form, and that is
what `probe_contract.json` declared and what the gate ran.

The reading is outcome-determining for the positive control:

| reading of "Adam + weight decay 1e-3" | certified code ROLE | NOVEL | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| coupled L2 inside `torch.optim.Adam` | 0.9167 | 0.6667 | fails |
| decoupled, as in `torch.optim.AdamW` | 0.9896 | 1.0000 | passes |
| weight decay 0 | 0.9896 | 1.0000 | passes |

Three candidate explanations were ruled out. Probe **initialization** is not the
cause: the declared Xavier-uniform/zero-bias scheme and the PyTorch default
`Linear` initialization give the same failing levels. Control **pooling** is not
the cause: both the declared summed 16-dimensional primary and the declared
32-dimensional canonical-concat secondary fail under the coupled reading. **Step
count** is not the cause: under the coupled reading NOVEL accuracy is flat from
500 through 12,000 steps.

The instrument's *discrimination* survives both readings. The non-automorphic
scramble stays near 0.16–0.20 ROLE and 0.08–0.13 NOVEL and the random control
stays at 0.0 under every reading. Only the absolute level of the positive control
moves across the 0.95 line. The frozen gate therefore mis-calibrates a working
instrument rather than catching a broken one.
