committed Issue 011 optimizer/evaluator. It did **not** rerun the two producer

1. `009.08`, the query-relative PP localization sweep and disposition; or

scientifically supported by its producer artifacts but independently provisional.
That distinction is consequential for Surface 13, which cannot inherit independent
closure from `012.02`.

This follow-up supplies the omitted execution checks. It does not edit or silently
reinterpret the frozen `012.02` report.
