## Why this turn exists

017.25 §B confessed a degeneracy in its own fixture and left it as an open item. This turn converts that confession into a measurement.

On the Fano successor `Σ′`, `Aut(Σ′)` is transitive on marked pairs and on unmarked pairs. So the derived class partition of `Point × Line` **is** the declared incidence relation together with its complement. Nothing was derived that was not already stated.

The consequence is sharper than 017.25 admitted, and it is now quantified. Define a **relation-indexed** learner: two parameters, indexed by membership in the declared relation, no compiler, no group, no derivation. Its exact asymptotic excess-log-loss floor on the Fano fixture is

```text
0.000000
```

and its exact expected curve is **identical to the compiled learner's at every sample size on the declared grid**. Every number in the 017.25 result is therefore equally consistent with a cheaper account:

> the advantage came from indexing parameters by a relation the schema already declares, and compilation contributed nothing.

017.25's headline stands as arithmetic but its *mechanism* claim is underdetermined. This turn is the experiment that decides between the two accounts.

## The change

### 1. A fixture where the quotient is not the relation

`Σ″` (`gpt-01724a-circulant-refinement-type-schema/v1`) declares a circulant incidence on seven points and seven lines with difference set `{0,1,2}`, matched to `Σ′` on every cardinality and deliberately not a projective plane.

| | `Σ′` Fano {0,1,3} | `Σ″` circulant {0,1,2} |
|---|---|---|
| points, lines, marked pairs, admitted inputs | 7, 7, 21, 49 | 7, 7, 21, 49 |
| `\|Aut\|` | 168 | 14 |
| derived classes | 2 — sizes 21, 28 | **4** — sizes 7, 14, 14, 14 |
| marked pairs split into | 21 | **7 + 14** |
| unmarked pairs split into | 28 | **14 + 14** |
| derived partition strictly refines the relation | no | **yes** |

Every derived class still lies wholly inside or wholly outside the relation — it must, since automorphisms preserve the relation — but on `Σ″` the partition is strictly finer. A learner holding only relation membership provably cannot reach the truth; one holding the derived quotient provably can. The matched cardinalities mean the contrast is not confounded by size.

`Σ″` declares its sorts, its 21 rows, the admitted inputs, the presentation equivalence, and a **binary** answer type with neutral labels `a₀`, `a₁`. Four classes do not mean four answers: a class carries a frequency of `a₀`, not an answer of its own. It declares no rule, no answer assignment, no frequency, and no preparation, effect, or ray coordinate.

### 2. Condition R — the cheaper account, named and measured

Two parameters indexed by the declared relation itself. On `Σ′` it is bit-identical to the compiled condition; on `Σ″` it carries a floor no amount of data removes. That single contrast is the measurement 017.24 could not make.

### 3. A declared-symmetry ladder — dose–response instead of A/B

Each rung is a distinct declaration of how much presentation equivalence the schema states. The compiler closes the declared generating set and reports the surviving free parameters.

```text
Σ″ circulant :  order  1 → 49 parameters
                order  7 →  7
                order 14 →  4   (the full automorphism group)

Σ′ Fano      :  order   1 → 49
                order   7 →  7
                order  21 →  3
                order 168 →  2
```

Every rung at least as fine as the truth has zero floor, so the rungs differ only in labeled-data burden and the prediction `dof/(2n)` is fixed in advance. A monotone measured curve is far stronger evidence of mechanism than any single contrast.

`Σ″` declares its two generators extensionally. The 017.20 Fano presentation does not use the circulant labeling, so its rung generators are derived from the compiled group by a deterministic rule — sort the group, greedily take the least element that grows the closure to the next requested order. Subgroups of a given order there are conjugate, so the class counts do not depend on the choice, and that independence is verified mechanically rather than asserted.

### 4. Condition U_relation — the strongest read-the-relation baseline

The uncompiled learner from 017.24 with the relation-membership bit appended to its 28 declared features. In 017.24 I proved the relation indicator was not in that learner's linear span, so it had to be discovered as an interaction. Here it is handed over outright. If T still separates from it on `Σ″`, the gain is not parameter economy over a readable relation.

### 5. A generalized exact reference

017.24's reference was specific to two classes with matched sampling weights. The replacement is exact for any number of classes **and for partitions too coarse for the truth**, which is what condition R and the coarse-compiler control require. It is also simpler: excess log loss is additive across cells, and a cell's estimator depends only on its own observation count, which is marginally binomial, so the expectation is a finite sum per cell with no joint lattice. It reproduces the 017.24 reference on the matched two-class case, and that agreement is a test.

### 6. Shared implementation, byte-pinned

The estimator, the hidden generator, the split builder, the exact evaluation, the uncompiled learner, and the scramble and erasure controls are imported byte-pinned from `../017.24-Code-attachments/training_economy.py` at SHA-256 `821d263a…`. Any difference in outcome is therefore attributable to the fixture and the new conditions, not to a reimplementation.

Three predictions are worth stating as predictions rather than burying in a table.

**Headline.** R is exactly indistinguishable from T on Fano at every sample size, and separated from T on the circulant by a floor of 0.0405 in Regime A and 0.0425 in Regime B that no amount of data removes.

**Dose–response.** Within each fixture, mean excess NLL at fixed `n` is monotone increasing in the number of free parameters surviving the declared symmetry, approaching `dof/(2n)`.

**Crossing, and it cuts against me.** On the circulant, R carries two parameters against T's four, so R is expected to beat T at `n = 4` only, with T ahead from `n = 8` onward. A later crossing, or an R advantage extending past `n = 16`, weakens the 017.25 story rather than strengthening it. I am recording the number before seeing it.

## Preregistered verdict rule

```text
SEPARATION ESTABLISHED   R equals T on Fano within sampling error, AND
                         R is separated from T on the circulant by more than
                         its exact floor less sampling error, AND
                         T reaches a threshold R never reaches on the grid, AND
                         the ladder is monotone in declared symmetry from n = 16

PARTIAL SEPARATION       R is separated on the circulant but a clause failed

NO SEPARATION            the relation-indexed learner remains an adequate
                         account, and the 017.25 mechanism claim is downgraded

IMPLEMENTATION DEFECT    T, O, and G disagree, or R and T differ on Fano where
                         they are the same partition and must be bit-identical
```

Even if every clause holds, the finding remains a statement about two finite 49-input fixtures. That ceiling is part of the preregistration.

## Controls

Inherited byte-pinned from 017.24 and recomputed for the circulant: the scrambled-structure ensemble, the relation-erased fail-closed check, and the wrong-compiler partition.

One control is new. A **coarse compiler** generates the world from the 7-parameter rung and then compiles at the full automorphism quotient — a legitimate group quotient that is nonetheless coarser than the world. Its exact floor is `0.057337` on both fixtures, against zero for the correct rung. The lesson it isolates: respecting *a* symmetry is not sufficient; the symmetry the schema declares has to be the one that generated the world.
