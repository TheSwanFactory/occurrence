## A. The two compilers

### `Q_ref` — fixed-point pair refinement over the admitted inputs

The nine items 017.28 §4 demands are defined in `Q_REF_SPECIFICATION` and emitted
verbatim into the certificate. In brief:

The **slot pool** of an ordered pair of admitted inputs (x, y) is the typed
components of x in declared signature order, then those of y, then every declared
constant, then — as one closure round — the value of every declared operation on
every type-correct tuple of the slots listed so far. The **atomic type** τ(x, y)
records which type-correct tuples of slot *positions* satisfy each declared
relation, and which same-sorted slot positions hold equal elements. Colours start
at τ(x, x). Each round refines to

    colour(x) ← ( colour(x), {{ (τ(x, y), colour(y)) : y ∈ X_Σ }} )

and stops when the class count stops rising, which for a monotone refinement means
the partition stopped changing. The round budget is 32; reaching it without a
fixpoint would be a recorded failure rather than a silent truncation.

Because the atomic type is a set of slot positions keyed by primitive
*declaration index*, no name and no element appears in it, so relabelling
invariance holds by construction and is additionally tested on every world.

This is the formalization of the procedure 017.27 disclosed at its own freeze. It
is reimplemented here independently, with a different encoding of the atomic type
— sets of satisfied positions rather than a vector of booleans — and the pinned
017.26 procedure is run alongside it on **all 87 searched worlds and both replay
worlds** and required to return the same partition. It did, in 89 of 89 cases.

### `Q_aut` — exact automorphism-orbit quotient

`Q_aut(Σ) = X_Σ / Aut(A_Σ)`, computed by the byte-pinned 017.26 compiler,
unchanged. Two exact routes are available; 017.27 established they agree, and the
agreement is re-established on this turn's worlds rather than inherited. The
route used per world is recorded.

`Q_aut` is the **exact reference** for this comparison because it has an intrinsic
mathematical characterization. Nothing here presumes it is the compiler a
production system should use.

### The predicted relation, verified rather than assumed

Every automorphism preserves every declared fact, so every atomic type is
automorphism-invariant, so `Q_ref` should be no finer than `Q_aut`. That is
checked on every searched world:

    x ~_aut y  ⇒  x ~_ref y     87 / 87 worlds, and 2 / 2 replay worlds

`Q_ref` never split an automorphism orbit. Had it done so, §8 says that is a
compiler defect and the turn stops.

## C. The frozen generator family `G`

Six families. The enumeration order is **computed** by a frozen rule rather than
written down: families sorted by declared primitive count, then declared
carrier-sort count, then name; within a family, parameter tuples sorted by total
declared carrier elements, then by the parameter tuple flattened to integers. The
declared tables are therefore only *sets* of parameters. What is checked
mechanically is that no two entries tie under the rule, so the computed order is
total, and that each family's declared profile is exactly what its builder
produces.

| # | family | sorts | primitives | §6 bullet | worlds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `circulant_graph` | 1 | 1 relation | regular graph constructions | 22 |
| 2 | `bipartite_incidence` | 2 | 1 relation | incidence constructions | 13 |
| 3 | `quasigroup` | 3 | 1 operation | typed maps / operations | 28 |
| 4 | `two_relation_graph` | 1 | 2 relations | multiple relations | 10 |
| 5 | `corridor` | 2 | relation + operation | distinguished source structure | 9 |
| 6 | `composite_product` | 4 | 2 operations + 1 relation | products / compositions | 5 |

**87 worlds proposed, 87 compiled, 0 skipped.** Declared bounds, readable off a
document before anything is compiled: `|X_Σ| ≤ 144`, total carrier elements ≤ 40,
world budget 200. The bounds were set by an **off-family tractability probe** —
shuffled and intercalate-turned cyclic Latin squares, and circulant graphs above
the bound — run before the family was frozen, so the bounds reflect compile cost
rather than compile results. No probe world is a member of `G`: the family's
quasigroups are lexicographically reduced squares, which the probe never
generated.

**No randomness anywhere.** The quasigroup family enumerates *reduced Latin
squares in lexicographic order* by backtracking, which is a construction, not a
sample. Order 4 has only four reduced squares in total, and the count is read off
the enumerator rather than assumed.

### The stated expectation, recorded here rather than discovered later

The family list carries an explicit a-priori hypothesis. The frozen `Q_ref` reads
only the declared facts of a four-to-eight slot pool, so a world whose declared
local data is constant everywhere is where it should go blind, and a Latin square
is the extreme case of that. That is why the quasigroup family is in `G`. Stating
the hypothesis in the freeze is preregistration; it is not the same thing as
choosing an instance after seeing the answer, and the computed ordering rule plus
the complete ledger are what make the difference auditable.

### Stop rule

Enumerate every world in the frozen order, compile both quotients on every world
within the declared bounds, record every outcome including equality cases. The
**first** separating world under the frozen order is the primary separation
fixture. The first separating world whose `Q_aut` is not the discrete partition is
additionally designated the graded separation fixture, because a world where the
exact compiler degenerates to one parameter per admitted input cannot show an
estimation trade-off. The family is not extended, reordered, or truncated after
any comparison is seen.

## E. First separation and exact witnesses

**Primary separation fixture: `quasigroup:n=6:square=1`** — the second reduced
Latin square of order 6 in lexicographic order.

```text
0 1 2 3 4 5
1 0 3 2 5 4
2 3 4 5 0 1
3 2 5 4 1 0
4 5 0 1 3 2
5 4 1 0 2 3
```

Declared: carriers `Row`(6), `Col`(6), `Sym`(6); one primitive
`op : Row × Col → Sym`, total, 36 rows; admitted inputs `Row × Col`, 36 of them.
Nothing else.

| | value |
|---|---|
| derived `\|Aut(A_Σ)\|` | **24** — the autotopism group of the square |
| `Q_aut` | **2 cells**, 12 / 24 |
| `Q_ref` | **1 cell**, 36 |
| `Q_ref` rounds to fixpoint | 1 |
| distinct atomic types | 7 |
| `Q_ref` strictly coarser than `Q_aut` | ✓ |

### The witness pair

    x = r0c0     y = r0c2

- x and y share refinement cell `R_0` — the only one there is
- x lies in orbit `A_0` (12 members), y in orbit `A_1` (24 members)
- **no structure-preserving map sends x to y.** `exists_morphism_sending`
  exhausts the whole product of symmetric groups under sound pruning and returns
  false, so the negative is exact rather than a search failure
- confirmed a second way: applying **every one of the 24 derived elements** to x
  never reaches y
- confirmed a third way, in the tests, by an algorithm that shares no code with
  the compiler: for a Latin square a choice of row and column bijection *forces*
  the symbol bijection, so the autotopism group can be enumerated outright. That
  independent enumeration returns order 24 and orbit sizes 12 / 24.

### Why the refinement was blind, stated as measured facts

Two facts, both checked mechanically:

1. **Every cell of this square lies in exactly one intercalate.** The only
   non-trivial declared fact the atomic type of a pair of cells can report on a
   Latin square is whether the two cells span a 2×2 Latin subsquare. On this
   square that count is 1 for every cell, so the whole admitted-input set carries
   one atomic profile and the fixpoint is one cell after one round.
2. **The orbit distinction is block agreement.** Writing the rows and columns in
   the three pairs {0,1}, {2,3}, {4,5}, orbit `A_0` is exactly the cells whose row
   pair and column pair coincide. That is a second-order property of the whole
   square, and the refinement's update does not compute it.

The second fact is diagnostic, not load-bearing: the orbit partition is derived
from declared structure alone, and naming the invariant afterwards only makes the
separation legible.

**The graded separation fixture coincides with the primary**, because the primary
already has a non-discrete `Q_aut`. One fixture is therefore designated for
Phase T. This is the *first* separation under the frozen order, not the widest;
`composite_product:n=10:m=5:offsets=1` has a 1-versus-13 gap and the frozen
protocol leaves it untested.

## F. Canonicality

Three senses, kept separate as §12 demands.

**Representation invariance.** Relabelling every carrier, element, and primitive,
and separately stripping every non-structural document key, leaves both compilers'
output identical. Because the relabelling renames positionally and preserves
declared order, the transported partition must be the *identical tuple*, which is
stronger than equality up to renaming. `Q_ref`: 87 / 87 worlds, both tests.
`Q_aut`: on the first world of every family and on the designated fixture, by a
declared rule rather than a per-world choice.

**Algorithmic determinism.** `Q_ref` recomputed twice in-process on every world:
identical. The whole certificate recomputed and required byte-identical, across
several `PYTHONHASHSEED` values. The certificate contains **no wall-clock number
and no floating-point measurement of any kind** — every timing lives in
`phase_s_cost_report.json` — which is what makes byte-identity achievable rather
than aspirational.

**Mathematical canonicity.** `Q_aut` has an intrinsic characterization: the orbit
partition of the full automorphism group of the declared structure, defined
without reference to any algorithm. `Q_ref` has no such characterization. It is
canonical only relative to the frozen construction above, and agreement with
`Q_aut` on 82 of 87 worlds does not transfer `Q_aut`'s canonicity to it.

## G. Structural cost

Measured wall clock over all 87 worlds; nothing extrapolated beyond the searched
sizes.

| | seconds |
|---|---|
| `Q_ref`, all 87 worlds | **1.42** |
| `Q_aut`, all 87 worlds | **55.36** |
| worst single world, `Q_aut` | **9.89** — `quasigroup:n=8:square=0` |

| family | `Q_ref` total | `Q_aut` total |
|---|---|---|
| `circulant_graph` (22 worlds, `\|X\|` 25–144) | 0.636 | 0.199 |
| `bipartite_incidence` (13) | 0.123 | 0.121 |
| `quasigroup` (28, `\|X\|` 16–64) | 0.205 | **54.98** |
| `two_relation_graph` (10) | 0.403 | 0.018 |
| `corridor` (9) | 0.034 | 0.021 |
| `composite_product` (5) | 0.021 | 0.020 |

The honest reading is not "exact compilation is slower". The **median** time ratio
is 0.82 — the exact compiler is often *faster*, because sound pruning is extremely
effective on a relation whose extension constrains a single carrier. The ratio's
**maximum is 627×**, and the whole of the exact compiler's cost is concentrated in
the 28 typed-operation worlds, where the search is over a product of three
symmetric groups. Within the quasigroup family the scaling is visible directly:
per-world `Q_aut` time is roughly 0.002 s at order 4, 0.008 s at 5, 0.067 s at 6,
0.61 s at 7, and 8.2 s at 8, against `Q_ref` at 0.0008 s to 0.017 s over the same
range.

## H. The compiler ladder, measured

    local atomic features
        ≤ bounded refinement, one round
        ≤ fixed-point refinement Q_ref
        ≤ exact automorphism orbit Q_aut

Every consecutive pair is checked with the refinement relation on every searched
world. The ladder held 87 / 87. Where each step is *strict*:

| step | strict on |
|---|---|
| bounded over atomic | 64 worlds |
| fixpoint over bounded | 27 worlds |
| **orbits over fixpoint** | **5 worlds** — exactly the separating ones |
| nothing strict | 19 worlds |

This is a measured fact about this declared vocabulary, not a universal total
ordering of compilers. Definability quotients, congruence closure,
bisimulation-like equivalence, context equivalence, task-relative observational
quotients, and canonical forms are named in the certificate and implemented
nowhere.

## I. Source counterfactuals on the primary fixture

Directions were frozen in the module before any perturbed world was compiled.

| counterfactual | `\|Aut\|` | `Q_ref` | `Q_aut` | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| erase the declared operation | 373,248,000 | 1 | 1 | **disappeared** |
| distinguish one declared `Row` element | 4 | 10 | 10 | **disappeared** |
| relabel every carrier | 24 | 1 | 2 | **persists**, identically |
| compose with a declared `Tag` map | 8 | 1 | 5 | **widens**, 1 → 4 |

The relabelling control changed nothing, so the gap is not a serialization
accident. It widens under a real composition, so it is a property of the typed
world. And it is *closable by ordinary source structure*: one declared reference
element — the kind of thing a domain specification records anyway — takes the two
compilers to the same 10-cell partition. That last row is the most practically
interesting thing in this table and it cuts against the stronger compiler.
