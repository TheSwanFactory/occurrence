## One-paragraph answer

The degeneracy 017.25 confessed is real, and it is total: on the Fano fixture the relation-indexed learner — two parameters keyed on membership in the declared relation, with no compiler, no group, and no derivation — produced **bit-identical predictions to the compiled TDM in 7,200 of 7,200 replicate comparisons**, and its mean excess held-out log loss matches to every reported decimal at all nine sample sizes. On that fixture 017.25's entire measured effect is explained without any derivation at all. On the circulant fixture, where the derived quotient strictly refines the declared relation, the same two learners coincide in only **20 of 7,200** comparisons, the relation-indexed learner plateaus at **0.0415** against its exactly predicted floor of **0.040527**, and it never reaches the 0.02 threshold that the compiled learner reaches at **128 labels**. The declared-symmetry dose–response is monotone at every sample size from 16 upward on both fixtures: at n = 1024 the Fano rungs give 0.0008, 0.0013, 0.0032, 0.0214 for 2, 3, 7, 49 surviving parameters, and the circulant rungs give 0.0019, 0.0034, 0.0212 for 4, 7, 49. Every one of the 54 predictions frozen and banked before the run is reproduced within four standard errors, worst deviation 3.44σ, zero failures — including the prediction that cut against the thesis, that the relation-indexed learner would win at n = 4 and only at n = 4. So compilation is load-bearing, and 017.25's mechanism claim survives the attack on it. Two things temper that. First, two post-freeze code corrections were required and are disclosed in full below; the per-replicate table is byte-identical across the second of them, which proves it changed no measurement. Second, the finding remains a statement about two finite 49-input fixtures, and on the Fano fixture — the one 017.25 actually used — the cheaper account remains exactly correct.

## A. What was at stake

017.25 §B said this about its own fixture:

> On this fixture `Aut(Σ′)` is transitive on flags and on antiflags, so the orbit partition of `Point × Line` **is** the declared relation together with its complement. Compilation is not discovering a hidden invariant; it is deciding, before Training, that the declared relation is the thing the model's parameters should be indexed by.

That was honest but incomplete, because it did not measure the consequence. This turn adds the condition that measures it. **R** carries two parameters indexed by relation membership and nothing else. If R matches the compiled condition T, then compilation contributed nothing measurable and 017.25's mechanism claim is unsupported on that fixture.

## C. The result on the circulant: the cheaper account fails

`Σ″` is matched to `Σ′` on all four cardinalities — 7 points, 7 lines, 21 marked pairs, 49 admitted inputs — but `|Aut| = 14` and the derived partition has four classes of sizes 7, 14, 14, 14, strictly refining the relation: the 21 marked pairs split 7 + 14 and the 28 unmarked pairs split 14 + 14.

R's curve flattens onto its exactly predicted floor of **0.040527** and stops. T's continues down as `4/(2n)`. The two coincide in only **20 of 7,200** comparisons, and those are small-`n` coincidences rather than structure.

T's 95% interval at n = 128 is [0.01377, 0.01609], below the threshold; at n = 64 it is [0.02362, 0.02726], above it. So 128 is the crossing, as frozen.

**The prediction that cut against the thesis was also correct.** I recorded before the run that R, having two parameters against T's four, should win at n = 4 and only at n = 4. Measured: R beats T at n = 4 (by 0.0053) and nowhere else. Fewer-but-wrong parameters do win in the very-low-data regime, and the frozen prediction said where the crossing would be.

## G. Controls

**Wrong compiler.** A partition with the derived cardinalities that respects no automorphism: 184 violated image pairs on the circulant, exact floor 0.054437, measured plateau 0.0564, never improving. Two parameters — or four — do not beat forty-nine. The right ones do.

**Coarse compiler, new this turn.** Generate the world from the 7-parameter rung, then compile at the full automorphism quotient: a *legitimate* group quotient that is nonetheless coarser than the world. Exact floor **0.057337** on both fixtures, against zero for the correct rung. Respecting a symmetry is not sufficient; the symmetry the schema declares has to be the one that generated the world.

**Scrambled structure.** No scramble of the circulant reproduces its 7/14/14/14 quotient. The scrambled compiler degrades toward the saturated model, 0.1534 → 0.0586.

**Relation erased.** Compiler and relational encoder both fail closed on the circulant, as on Fano.

## H. What the uncompiled learners did

Condition `U_relation`, new this turn, is handed the relation-membership bit outright instead of having to discover it as an interaction. It did **not** help: on the circulant it runs 1.3106 → 0.0292 across the grid, worse than `U_quadratic` at 0.7268 → 0.0234. Being handed the relation does not rescue a learner that must still find the refinement, and the extra feature costs it capacity. At n = 1024 its internal selection picks the relational MLP in 100 of 100 replicates, so the variant was exercised rather than ignored.

`U_best` never reaches 0.02 on the circulant in either regime. Conditions O and G remain bit-identical to T on both fixtures across all 14,400 comparisons, so nothing in this turn revives OT as the source of the advantage. The mechanism is still structural compilation, exactly as 017.25 concluded — this turn only shows that the compilation is doing real work rather than restating the relation.

## K. Post-freeze corrections, disclosed

The freeze was committed and banked at `occurrence/gpt@adbc039c` before the run. Two corrections were then required. Both are auditable against that banked text.

**1. The inherited wrong-compiler control assumed two classes.** `build_wrong_partition` in the 017.24 module hardcodes the 21/28 split and raises on a four-class fixture. The run failed immediately. It was generalized *in this turn's module* rather than in the byte-pinned shared one, so 017.25's artifacts remain valid: move the `k` lexicographically least members of each derived class into the next class cyclically, with one common `k`, preserving every cardinality. On a two-class fixture with `k = 7` it reproduces the 017.24 construction **member for member** — same 3,332 violations, same 0.106784 floor — which is asserted mechanically, so the correction cannot have loosened the control.

**2. The dose–response monotonicity check compared in the wrong direction.** The banked preregistration predicts mean excess NLL "monotone **increasing** in the number of free parameters surviving the declared symmetry". The code compared adjacent rungs the other way round relative to its own ascending sort, so a perfectly monotone result was reported as a violation and the first completed run returned PARTIAL SEPARATION. The data was monotone all along: 0.0008 < 0.0013 < 0.0032 < 0.0214. The comparison was corrected and a regression test now pins the direction against the banked preregistration text.

That second correction changed verdict evaluation only, and this is checkable rather than asserted: `refinement_per_replicate.csv.gz` hashes to `ee28d2fd…` both before and after it, and `refinement_certificate.json` and `generalized_reference.json` are likewise byte-identical. **No measurement changed.** Neither correction touches a primary condition, a fixture, an estimator, or any of the 54 frozen predictions.

A reader who wants to check that I did not move the target should diff this turn's module against the freeze commit `d88c478` and read the two changes against `preregistration.json` as banked at `adbc039c`.
