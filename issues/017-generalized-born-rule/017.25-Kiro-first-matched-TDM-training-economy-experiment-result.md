## One-paragraph answer

Typing the input world removes work that Training would otherwise have to do, and the size of the effect is large: the compiled two-parameter TDM reaches mean excess held-out log loss ≤ 0.02 at **64 labeled examples** in both regimes and at all three ground truths, while no preregistered information-parity uncompiled learner reaches that threshold anywhere on the 4–1024 grid, and neither does the saturated 49-parameter ablation. That is a label-burden ratio of at least 32× under the preregistered factor-two criterion, and it survives a deliberately illegitimate robustness check in which the baseline is handed its hindsight-optimal regularization. But the same experiment denies the flattering reading of that result. Condition **O**, which is given the derived class identifier and never invokes OT, the Born formula, or the compiler, produces **bit-identical predictions to T in all 21,600 replicate comparisons**; so does condition **G**, a generic symmetry learner that is handed the automorphism group and closes the admitted inputs under it by union-find without ever naming a quotient. The advantage is therefore entirely attributable to **structural compilation of the input space before Training** — exactly the outcome §6 of the task anticipated and called important rather than a failure — and not to the Born rule, the invariant state family, or anything else OT-specific. The three controls confirm that the gain tracks *correct* source structure rather than mere parameter economy: a wrong two-cell partition with the identical 21/28 cardinalities plateaus at an exact positive floor of **0.1068** and never improves, the scrambled-structure ensemble reproduces the declared quotient in **0 of 20** cases and yields groups of order 1, 2, or 4 with 35 to 49 classes, and the relation-erased schema makes the compiler and the relational encoder both fail closed. The simulation is validated against an exact binomial-lattice reference in all 54 cells, and that reference independently predicted the whole T curve before any label was drawn: **E[excess NLL] = d/(2n) = 1/n**. One disclosure is load-bearing: the uncompiled baseline was **strengthened after a pilot** — from two variants to five, with a nested complexity ladder, a ridge grid, matched intercept shrinkage, and 100 replicates — because the originally preregistered version was worse than a one-parameter constant predictor, which is an optimization failure rather than an information limit. Every amendment moved against the thesis, and none was chosen by comparing evaluation outputs.

## A. Schema

`Σ′` (`gpt-01724-fano-successor-type-schema/v1`, SHA-256 `149778bf…`) declares exactly what 017.23 §J required and nothing more.

**Declared:**

```text
sort Point, cardinality 7
sort Line,  cardinality 7
the 21-member incident relation
admitted inputs Point × Line, cardinality 49
raw-presentation equivalence under incidence automorphisms
a binary answer type with the neutral labels a₀, a₁
```

**No longer declared.** The single top-level key dropped relative to the 017.20 predecessor is `decision_type`, and with it the total rule `Return incident iff the input pair belongs to the declared incident relation`. `Σ′` carries no per-input answer, no statement that incidence determines an answer, no λ, no preparation coordinate, no effect coordinate, no ambient ray, no central orientation, and no decision procedure of any kind.

The precondition 017.23 called sharp is therefore met: the answers supplied to Training are **not derivable from `Σ′`**. Structure comes from the schema; statistics come from data.

Absence is enforced structurally, not by assertion. Five mechanical checks: no forbidden key (`rule`, `answer`, `lambda`, `probability`, `target`, `preparation`, `effect`, `ray`, `orientation`, …) occurs anywhere in `Σ′`; `Σ′` contains no float at all; no array in `Σ′` mixes a sort identifier with an answer label; the carrier handed to the compiler is a dataclass whose only fields are `points`, `lines`, `incidence`, `opaque_rows`, so it **cannot represent** answer semantics; and no fitting function in the module accepts a parameter named for the target. The leakage scan is shown non-vacuous by poisoning a copy of `Σ′` with a rule and with a mixed array and confirming both are caught.

## B. Compilation

The compiler reads `sorts` and `relations`. It derives, with no label in scope:

```text
|Aut(Σ′)|                          168        (declared expectation: 168)
classes of Point × Line               2        (declared expectation: 2)
cardinalities                    21, 28        (declared expectation: 21, 28)
canonical names               O₀ = 21, O₁ = 28
```

Ordering is by cardinality then least member, so the names are bookkeeping. The 21-member class coincides with the predecessor's marked pairs; that correspondence is recorded for the reader and is never supplied to any learner.

`Σ′` states the group order and class sizes as **redundant self-checks**. They are load-free: deleting both blocks and recompiling reproduces the identical certificate, which is checked mechanically. The compiler derives them from the relation and then agrees with them.

One structural remark worth stating plainly, because it bounds how much the compiler is doing. On this fixture `Aut(Σ′)` is transitive on flags and on antiflags, so the orbit partition of `Point × Line` **is** the declared relation together with its complement. Compilation is not discovering a hidden invariant; it is deciding, before Training, that the declared relation is the thing the model's parameters should be indexed by. That is a smaller claim than "derives structure", and it is the honest one.

## C. Learned residue

The hypothesis family is the certified one, unchanged:

```text
z_M = (I₂, 0)          z_s = (0, 1)
ω_λ(A,s) = λ·Tr(A)/2 + (1−λ)·s,   λ ∈ [0,1]
C_θ(x)  = ω_{λ_{o(x)}}
P(a₀|x) = λ_{o(x)}
```

Exactly **two** observable learned scalars, θ = (λ₀, λ₁) ∈ [0,1]². The complementary orientation is the exact reparameterization θ ↦ 1−θ and is verified to be an involution of the public family on a 25-point grid; it is never a third parameter and never a training target.

T's predictions are produced by *evaluating the TDM*, not by returning the estimate. Each per-class estimate is pushed through `ω_λ` and the intrinsic central test, and the result is resolved through the shipped `decision-model` contracts — `Test`, `resolve_test`, `Distribution` — at **zero tolerance** on a 25-point λ grid and on all 49 admitted inputs. Nothing was added to that package's 12-symbol public API.

## D. Fairness

| Condition | Receives | Withheld | Learned scalars |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** typed compiled TDM | all of `Σ′`; labels | θ\*, answers not in data | 2 |
| **O** oracle class | the derived class id per observation; labels | the compiler, OT, the Born formula, θ\* | 2 |
| **U** uncompiled | point identity, line identity, the query point's incidence row over all 7 lines, the query line's incidence column over all 7 points; labels | the class id, the quotient, any same-class or adjacency feature of the query pair, θ\* | 29 – 481 |
| **S** saturated | pair identity; labels | everything structural | 49 |
| **G** generic symmetry | the automorphism group as permutations; labels | the class quotient itself, θ\*, OT | 2 (derived) |

Every condition is refitted from the **same per-input sufficient counts** of the **same observations** under the **same splits** — enforced structurally, since the counts are computed once per replicate and shared.

Two constructive certificates fix U's position precisely, because the credibility of the whole result depends on U being neither starved nor spoon-fed.

**Not starved.** An explicit weight setting makes U compute the class function *exactly*: seven hidden units `relu(row_k(point) + onehot_k(line) − 1)` sum to the marked-pair indicator, so the compiled family lies inside U's hypothesis class. A second explicit setting isolates a single admitted pair, so U is not restricted to class-invariant functions and the two-class structure is nowhere imposed as a parameter tying rule.

**Not spoon-fed.** Every one of U's 28 feature columns is a function of the query point alone or of the query line alone, so the entire column span consists of additive functions `f(point) + g(line)`. Any additive function has vanishing alternating sum on a 2×2 block of inputs. The indicator does not: on `{P0,P1} × {L0,L1}` its corners are `1, 1, 1, 0` with alternating sum `−1`. So the class indicator is neither a coordinate nor a linear combination of coordinates — least-squares residual `0.5714` — and U must discover it as an interaction while holding the complete declared relation.

`U_quadratic`, the degree-two expansion, is the exception and is labeled as such wherever it appears: its span **does** contain the class structure, because the indicator equals `Σₖ rowₖ(point)·onehotₖ(line)`. It is included deliberately so the headline comparison runs against the strongest information-parity baseline short of handing the quotient over. It is also, in the event, the variant that `U_best` selects in 17 of 18 primary cells.

### Baseline amendment, disclosed

A pilot on a reduced grid showed the originally preregistered U (fixed 600-step budget, ridge 1e-3, no model selection) was **worse than a one-parameter constant predictor** whose floor is 0.1283. That is an optimization and regularization failure, not an information limit, and §14 of the task makes the competence of the baseline decisive. The U family was therefore enlarged before the final replicate bank:

```text
variants        2 → 5        added U_selected, U_quadratic, U_ladder
step budget   600 → 1200
ridge grid      — → {1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1, 1, 10}, selected on training labels only
candidates      — → nested ladder: constant(1), linear(29), quadratic(407), mlp(481)
shrinkage       — → the ridge now covers intercepts, so high penalty shrinks to
                    p = ½, matching the beta(1,1) prior mean T uses
replicates     30 → 100
```

Every change strengthens the baseline and therefore works against the thesis. No hyperparameter value was selected by comparing evaluation outputs; the grids are round numbers fixed a priori and consumed by a selection procedure that sees training labels only. The pilot's outputs are not reported as results.

To make the amendment auditable rather than merely asserted, the artifacts include a **fairness frontier**: U's whole regularization path, trained on all n with no internal split, with the ridge chosen by hindsight against the evaluation metric. This is explicitly *not* a legitimate baseline and does not decide the verdict. It shows that at the top of the ridge grid every candidate converges to the constant-model floor, so the grid has reached the achievable frontier rather than being truncated short of it — `grid_is_adequate` holds in all 18 cells — and that the measured gap is not an optimization artifact.

## E. Analytic expectation

Before any empirical run, the two pooled Bernoulli estimators are independent given the class sample counts, and the counts are binomial, so the entire expectation is a finite double sum. Computed exactly over the lattice, with no simulation:

```text
E[excess NLL] = Σ_c  eval_w_c / (2 n train_w_c)  +  O(n⁻²)
```

With matched weights and two classes this is exactly `d/(2n) = 1/n`, independent of θ\* and of the 21/28 split. Regime B gives `≈ 0.9907/n` from the 17/22 seen against 4/6 held-out weights.

| n | exact expected excess NLL | d/(2n) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 0.086243 | 0.250000 |
| 32 | 0.026482 | 0.031250 |
| 64 | 0.014500 | 0.015625 |
| 256 | 0.003837 | 0.003906 |
| 1024 | 0.000972 | 0.000977 |

So the prediction available in advance was: T crosses 0.02 near n ≈ 50 and 0.01 near n ≈ 100, landing on grid points 64 and 128. It does. **All 54 cells** agree with the exact reference within four standard errors, worst deviation 3.47σ, zero failures. The observed advantage is the one the exact two-parameter statistics already predicted, not an optimizer artifact — which is what §13 asked to be ruled out.

## G. Regime B — held-out input identity

This is the crucial transfer test, and the separation is qualitative rather than quantitative. Each replicate holds out 10 whole `Point × Line` identities, stratified 4 from O₀ and 6 from O₁, and trains only on observations from the 39 seen identities.

| condition | n=4 | 64 | 256 | 1024 | n→0.02 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T** typed | 0.0933 | 0.0150 | 0.0037 | 0.0010 | **64** |
| **O / G** | 0.0933 | 0.0150 | 0.0037 | 0.0010 | 64 |
| **S** saturated | 0.1308 | 0.1308 | 0.1308 | 0.1308 | — |
| **U_best** | 0.6221 | 0.2344 | 0.2500 | 0.2553 | — |
| *U_oracle_ridge* | 0.1307 | 0.1294 | 0.1287 | 0.1273 | — |

Three things are visible.

**T transfers immediately and exactly.** Its exact asymptotic floor on unseen identities is 0.000000, and its Regime B curve is indistinguishable from its Regime A curve. A compiled class parameter is defined on the class, so an identity never seen in Training is already covered.

**S cannot transfer at all.** Its excess is pinned at exactly 0.130812 — the exact floor — with **zero variance across all 400 replicates at every sample size**. A pair-specific learner has no mechanism to say anything about a pair it has not seen; it falls back on the prior, forever.

**U transfers worse than S, and gets worse with more data.** `U_best` rises from 0.2344 at n=64 to 0.2553 at n=1024. This is not a bug and it is worth stating clearly: S at least *abstains* on an unseen identity, returning ½, whereas U extrapolates confidently from identity features it has fitted, and a confident wrong answer costs more log loss than an honest one. Its calibration error at n=256 is 0.2332 against T's 0.0295.

Per-class transfer at n=256: T 0.0044 on the four held-out O₀ identities and 0.0033 on the six held-out O₁ identities; `U_best` 0.3147 and 0.2069. Brier at n=256: T 0.1889, S 0.2500, `U_best` 0.3019.

## H. Controls

**Wrong compiler.** A two-cell partition with the *same* 21 and 28 cardinalities, built by exchanging the 7 lexicographically least members of each true class, violates the declared automorphisms in 3,332 of the checked image pairs and mixes both cells (14+7 and 7+21). Against data generated from the true class structure it plateaus at 0.1077 in Regime A, against an exact predicted floor of 0.106784, and never improves with more labels. **This is the control that matters most**, because it is the one that refutes the trivial explanation "two parameters beat forty-nine". Two parameters do not beat forty-nine. The *right* two parameters do.

**Scrambled structure.** Twenty deterministic relabelings, each preserving 7 point labels, 7 line labels, 21 marked pairs, and 49 admitted pairs. The compiler is recomputed from each scrambled schema rather than reusing the declared quotient. Groups come out of order 1, 2, or 4; class counts 35, 36, 42, or 49. **None of the 20 reproduces the 21/28 quotient**, and no two-class result was forced anywhere. The reference scramble has |Aut| = 2 and 42 classes, so the typed learner degenerates toward the saturated model: 0.0399 at n=1024 in Regime A, and in Regime B it *degrades* with more data, 0.1323 → 0.1712, because it confidently learns 42 cell values that do not transfer. The typed advantage follows the structure actually declared.

**Relation erased.** Both sorts, all 49 admitted pairs, all 21 rows as opaque payload, and the answer labels survive; only the relational meaning of the rows is removed. The compiler **fails closed** with `cannot enumerate automorphisms without a declared relation`, and so does the relational encoder. There is no fallback path, so T cannot silently recover the historical quotient from label positions.

Compilation is not free, and the accounting is separated as §8 required.

Derivation is computed **once** per Type Schema, cached, and reused across every sample size and replicate: one derivation served 100,800 model fits. At roughly 101 µs per closed-form fit, the 4.1 ms derivation pays for itself after about 40 fits and is thereafter free. For a single fit it would dominate.

This is a property of a 49-input fixture and is **not** a production economics claim. The honest statement is narrower: on this fixture the compiler's cost is negligible relative to the Training it replaces, and the interesting quantity — labeled-data burden — moved by a factor of at least 32.

## J. Verdict

**STRONG POSITIVE** on the task's own §14 definition, with the mechanism identified and the credit correctly assigned.

The preregistered criterion was a factor of two or more fewer labeled examples to reach mean excess NLL ≤ 0.02, required in both regimes at the primary θ\*.

```text
T and O agree                                    exactly, 21,600/21,600 replicates
T and G agree                                    exactly, 21,600/21,600 replicates
T labels to 0.02                                 64   (regime A and regime B)
U_best labels to 0.02                            not reached on the 4–1024 grid
label ratio under the preregistered convention   ≥ 32×,  material in both regimes
S labels to 0.02                                 not reached;  ratio ≥ 32×
U_oracle_ridge (robustness only)                 not reached;  ratio ≥ 32×
advantage when the same structure is supplied     vanishes: G ≡ T bit-for-bit
```

The final clause of §14's strong-positive condition — "the advantage disappears or shrinks sharply when U/G discovers or is given the same structural quotient" — is satisfied in its strongest form. G is handed the group, not the quotient, closes the inputs under it by a generic union-find, and lands on **exactly** T's predictions.

That is why the disposition names the mechanism. The measured gain is **structural compilation of the input space before Training**. It is not the Born rule, not the invariant state family, and not anything OT-specific: O reaches the identical numbers with no OT machinery at all, and G reaches them from a bare permutation group. §6 of the task anticipated precisely this and called it an important result rather than a failure. It is the result.

The strongest claim these data justify is the one the task itself drafted:

> On this finite controlled problem, a rigorously typed source structure can compile a sufficient input quotient before Training, reducing the observable learned family from per-instance parameters to two shared probabilities and, as the matched baselines confirm, reducing labeled-data burden.

Two qualifications belong in the same breath. First, the advantage is measured against *these five* preregistered uncompiled learners; it is not a proof that no generic learner could close the gap, and the fairness frontier is published so that claim can be attacked directly. Second, on this fixture the compiled quotient is the declared relation itself, so "compiling structure" here means indexing parameters by a relation the schema already states — a real and measurable saving, and a smaller one than the phrase might suggest.

## Forbidden conclusions, restated

Even on a strong positive, none of the following is established, and none is claimed here: language-scale sample-efficiency gains; superiority to LLMs or SLMs; physical realization of the OT test; autonomous dynamics; that every useful Type Schema admits a compact compiler; that OT is uniquely responsible for the gain — the experiment actively refutes that last one.
