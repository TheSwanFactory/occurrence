## B. What the compiler is, and how "generic" is made auditable

The compiler consumes a primitive **by its declared kind and signature**. The rule table is the only place it branches, and it reads:

- relation `R ⊆ C₁ × ⋯ × Cₙ`:  `R(a₁,…,aₙ) ⇔ R(g_{C₁}(a₁),…,g_{Cₙ}(aₙ))`
- operation `f : C₁ × ⋯ × Cₙ → B`:  `g_B(f(a₁,…,aₙ)) = f(g_{C₁}(a₁),…,g_{Cₙ}(aₙ))`
- constant `c : C`:  `g_C(c) = c`

It then derives `Aut(A_Σ)` by exhaustive backtracking over the full product of symmetric groups, rejecting a branch only where a declared obligation is already violated, and derives

    q_Σ : X_Σ → X_Σ / Aut(A_Σ)

by asking, for each ordered pair of admitted inputs, whether some derived map carries one to the other. That question never materialises the group, which is why the erased world below stays tractable at `|Aut| = 25,401,600`.

017.26b §3.1 forbids fixture dispatch. A source scan is the weak form of that check and is reported, but two **behavioural** tests are the binding form:

- **Relabelling.** Rename every carrier, every element, and every primitive to meaningless codes. The derived group order and the derived quotient are identical.
- **Key stripping.** Delete every document key outside `sorts`, `primitives`, `admitted_inputs`, and delete every prose description. The derived group order and the derived quotient are identical.

So no metadata anywhere else in either document can be load-bearing, whatever it says, and the compiler cannot be recognising a fixture it knows. The tests additionally hand the same compiler three worlds that exist nowhere in the repository — a 4-cycle, the same 4-cycle with a declared constant, and a fresh three-carrier world with a typed map — and check its output against unpruned brute force.

That is the 017.25a separation, now reached without being handed a single generator.

### Maximality, argued in layers rather than asserted

1. The search rejects only genuine violations, so it cannot discard a structure-preserving map.
2. Every element it returns is re-verified by an independent checker that re-derives the rules from the signature.
3. Unpruned brute force over the whole product of symmetric groups on a reduced world (576 candidates for A, 1,440 for B) returns the **same element set**.
4. A second exhaustive decomposition — enumerate one carrier's permutations outright, complete the rest — returns the **same order**.
5. Composing every derived element with every transposition of every carrier exhausts Cayley distance one: **0 of 588** and **0 of 234** neighbours preserve every primitive.
6. Uniform sampling of 200,000 elements of the product found nothing preserving every primitive. With `14 / 25,401,600` that is expected, so this is reported as the weak witness it is.
7. The quotient is derived twice, by pair query and by union-find over the materialised group, and the two agree.

## D. The frozen shortcut language Lₖ, and its bound

017.26b §4 replaces "cheap statistic" with a preregistered finite language. Lₖ is generated mechanically at **k = 2** from the declared primitives: typed terms over the input components and declared constants closed under the declared operations; every relation on every type-correct term tuple; every type-correct equality between distinct terms; a local count feature for every relation, argument position, and type-correct filling of the other positions; a fibre-size count for every operation and every term of its codomain; and the equality and order comparisons between count features.

| | Fixture A | Fixture B |
|---|---|---|
| terms | 2 — `x0`, `x1` | 3 — `x0`, `x1`, `district(x0)` |
| `|Lₖ|` | 5 | 18 |
| distinct induced partitions | 2 | 2 |
| finest single feature | 21 / 28 | 9 / 18 |
| **whole language read jointly** | **21 / 28** | **9 / 18** |
| recovers `q_Σ` | **no** | **no** |

Two deliberate strengthenings over the letter of §4. Raw count values are kept as features rather than only the equality tests between them. And the **joint** partition of the whole language is reported, which bounds the entire Boolean closure without enumerating it: any negation, conjunction, or disjunction of these atoms induces a partition coarser than or equal to their joint.

A test checks directly that every Lₖ feature takes the same value on an input and on its image under every derived map. That is what makes the verdict mean what it says: an Lₖ feature can only be **coarser than or equal to** `q_Σ`, never crosswise to it, so "does not recover" is exactly "is strictly coarser".

### The bound, disclosed now rather than later

017.26b §4.1 requires the bound to be named. Naming it is not enough, so the stronger adversary is implemented and its result is banked here:

> An iterated pair refinement over the admitted inputs — colours seeded by an input's own declared facts, refined by the multiset over every other input of the facts the pair jointly satisfies — **recovers `q_Σ` exactly on both fixtures**, in two iterations.

This is disclosed at the freeze, before θ* exists, so it cannot later be presented as a caveat discovered once the numbers were visible. Three things follow, and no more.

- It is **not** a member of Lₖ, which is source-local and depth-bounded by construction. Lₖ's result stands.
- 017.26b §8 forbids condition R from computing "an equivalent global structural quotient", which is precisely what this is. R is therefore correctly fenced from it by the contract.
- It does **not** make the derivation cheaper *in kind*, because it is itself a deterministic pre-label structural derivation rather than a statistic a learner reads off an input. It makes it cheaper *in cost*. That is a tractability finding and it is reported as one, and it bears directly on the 017.26b §12 open item about whether `Q_aut` is the right compiler.

## E. Source perturbations, with directions frozen before compilation

017.26b §6 asks for the **direction** of change, declared before the perturbed world is compiled. The directions below were written into the module and committed; every perturbed world is recompiled from scratch through the same generic path.

| Fixture | Perturbation | Frozen direction | `|Aut|` | cells | held |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | erase `incident` | grows | 25,401,600 | 1 | ✓ |
| A | scramble `incident` | differs | 1 | 49 | ✓ |
| A | add constant in `Point` | shrinks | 2 | 25 | ✓ |
| B | erase `adjacent` | grows | 1,296 | 2 | ✓ |
| B | erase `district` | grows | 108 | 1 | ✓ |
| B | scramble `adjacent` | differs | 1 | 27 | ✓ |
| B | scramble `district` | differs | 1 | 27 | ✓ |
| B | add constant in `District` | shrinks | 2 | 14 | ✓ |

Both symmetry breaks yield a **subgroup** of the original group, checked by set inclusion, and a **refinement** of the original quotient — the `Aut(A_Σ⁺) ⊆ Aut(A_Σ)` prediction of §6. A scramble preserves every superficial count — the same carriers, the same number of rows, the same fibre-size multiset — and still destroys the quotient, which is the evidence that the compiler reads structure rather than cardinality.

### Joint load-bearing

017.26b §5 requires the richer fixture to demonstrate that its primitives are jointly load-bearing. Deleting `adjacent` takes `|Aut|` from 6 to 1,296 and the quotient from 5 cells to 2. Deleting `district` takes it from 6 to 108 and from 5 cells to 1. Each primitive strictly enlarges the group and strictly coarsens the quotient when removed, so neither is decoration. Fixture A has one primitive and therefore **cannot** earn a Strong positive on its own, exactly as the contract says.
