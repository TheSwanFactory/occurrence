### One correction to the advisory's one-line rule

`009.09a` section 1 offers the nonlearned ceiling as one line:

```text
forced_third(a, b | anchor) = the common neighbour c of {a, b}
                              such that |{a, b, c} ∩ anchor| = 1
```

That is exactly right on the domain it names — the nine **scored** queries, where the
anchor contributes at most one of `a`, `b`. It was verified to reproduce the certified
third on every scored pair of every anchor of every habitat.

Section 6 additionally queries the three **support** pairs in order to reconstruct the
whole four-chamber relation, and there `{a, b}` already lies inside the anchor, so the
correct answer meets the anchor in **three** tokens, not one. The form covering all
twelve admitted pairs with one predicate is odd parity:

```text
forced_third(a, b | anchor) = the common neighbour c of {a, b}
                              such that |{a, b, c} ∩ anchor| is ODD
```

Both are implemented, the `= 1` form is carried alongside as `anchor_third_unit_form`,
and their agreement on the nine scored pairs is a recorded law. Under parity the exact
ceiling is `1.000` on the scored queries and `1.000` on all twelve; under `= 1` it is
`1.000` on the scored queries and `0.750` on all twelve, because it returns no answer
at all on the three support pairs. That gap is the first sign of the section 6.1
tension section 9 below makes precise.

## 5. Architecture and the anti-identity ledger

The entire input surface, and there is nothing else:

```text
marks       (B, 6, 2)   [anchor_mark, query_mark] per node, values in {0.0, 1.0}
adjacency   (B, 6, 6)   the symmetric ADMIT matrix, zero diagonal, {0.0, 1.0}
```

```text
input_dim                                      2
values_are_binary                           True
adjacency_is_symmetric                      True
adjacency_diagonal_is_zero                  True
adjacency_edges                               12
anchor_marked_nodes                            3
query_marked_nodes                             2
embedding tables                               0
buffers                                        0
no Event / habitat / slot index is an input  True
```

`009.08` supplied candidate chamber tokens as a frozen buffer. This model has no
buffer at all: the candidates *are* the six current nodes, and everything the learner
can say about them comes from the relation graph and the two marks.

Message passing with sum aggregation over admitted edges, then a shared per-node
readout emitting one scalar per node:

```text
h      = Embed(marks)
h      = ReLU( Update_l( [h , Aggregate_l(h, Adj)] ) )      l = 1..2
score  = Readout(h)                                          -> (B, 6)
```

Sum aggregation is the load-bearing choice: the message is a nonlinear map of a node's
two mark bits, so summing it over a node's admitted neighbours yields the *counts* of
each mark type in that neighbourhood, which is the relational information the anchor
rule turns on.

The argmax is held to **exact** covariance with no tolerance on every *decisive* row —

forced rather than excused. All 80 did. The ties occur only on the untrained model the
audit probes; the scores agree to round-off because permuting the node order reorders a
float sum and addition is not associative.

### The pathology, localized before it was repaired

Split the nine scored queries by how many of the two query nodes the anchor contains.
The anchor is a triangle, so:

```text
anchor_overlap = 1   exactly one query node is anchor-marked    6 of 9
anchor_overlap = 0   neither query node is anchor-marked        3 of 9
```

The exact rule needs `|{a, b, c} ∩ anchor|` **odd**, which means "pick the common
neighbour *not* in the anchor" when overlap is 1 and "pick the common neighbour *in*
the anchor" when overlap is 0. The anchor preference flips sign between the two cases.
The first half alone scores `6/9 = 0.6667`, and it is close to linear in features the
candidate already has.

```text
primary block, one_anchor, per-cell forced-third histogram
LOHO   n = 112     0.666667  x 81     0.777778  x 1     1.000000  x 30
LOFPO  n =  56     0.613426  x  1     0.666667  x 40    1.000000  x 15

primary block, one_anchor, LOHO means
forced_third_accuracy                        0.7569
train_accuracy                               0.7569
generalization_gap                          -0.0000
anchor_overlap_1_accuracy                    1.0000
anchor_overlap_0_accuracy                    0.2708
```

Three things make the diagnosis mechanical rather than inferred. Train and test agree
to four decimals with a generalization gap of `-0.0000079492`, so the model has not
overfitted — it has not fitted. The failing cells sit at exactly `2/3` with
`anchor_overlap_1 = 1.0000` and `anchor_overlap_0 = 0.0000`, which is the majority-case
rule and nothing else. And the failure is **cell-dependent**, not seed-dependent: every
one of the 8 seeds has both saddle cells and clean cells, so 81 of 112 LOHO cells and
41 of 56 LOFPO cells fell in.

Two alternative explanations were tested and rejected before any repair was chosen.
Those probes were a **pre-repair diagnostic**, recorded as prose in
`discovery_heads.DIAGNOSED_PATHOLOGY` rather than as a checked artifact, and they are
labelled as such: with the step count varied to 4000 the affected cells never leave
`0.6667`, and with depth varied to 3 layers accuracy reaches `1.0000` by step 200 and
*falls back* to `0.6667` by step 800 while 4 layers oscillates. What carries weight is
in the sweep artifact itself and needs no appeal to those probes: 30 of 112 LOHO cells
reach `1.0000` at the primary depth and the declared step count, so the parity rule is
representable there; train equals test to four decimals in the failing cells; and every
one of the 8 seeds contains both failing and succeeding cells. It is a flat attractor,
not a capacity limit.

The cause: with `m_ij = Message(h_j)` the candidate must learn the conjunction
"neighbour is query-marked **and** anchor-marked" inside the message function and only
then combine that count with its own anchor mark. The parity is assembled from two
separately learned pieces and the linearly available piece already scores `6/9`.

### The repair, and only this


optimizer, learning rate, step count, seeds, arms, folds, episodes, relabelings and

conjunction of the candidate's own mark with its neighbour's marks available to a
single hidden layer.

### The support-pair regime is unsupervised, and its answer is underdetermined

This is not an interpretive complaint. It is computed.

Section 4.4 makes the three pairs inside the anchor *support* rather than scored
queries, and section 5.3 permits supervision only on query pairs, so no training row
ever has `|{a, b} ∩ anchor| = 2`. Two rules over the supplied atoms were exhibited and
checked exhaustively over all 14 habitats, 4 anchors and 12 admitted pairs:


The two rules are indistinguishable on all 6048 rows the task permits training on and

**Why B is filed and not A.** Sections 6.1 to 6.3 define the three chart metrics over a
reconstruction from all twelve admitted pairs, and section 8's thresholds refer to
those definitions. Section 9 class E names "stop rather than repair the scientific
question after results are visible". Adopting the supplied-anchor reconstruction

full and offered to the Owner instead of substituted.

**Why class B's stated interpretation does not hold.** Section 9 reads a chart-audit
failure as "the learner found a useful query rule, not the claimed representation".
That reading is contradicted by the evidence. Under the supplied-anchor reconstruction
the learner's own answers imply three of the four chambers, the fourth is the anchor
the task supplies as support, the resulting four-chamber system **is** the certified
cyclic-block family at `0.9911`, and the exact `AGL(2,2)` search confirms every one of
them is equivalent to the hidden SFP chart. What fails is the three support-pair
answers, and section 9 above proves those are underdetermined by the task's own
supervision rule. So the honest statement is not "a query rule rather than a
representation"; it is "a representation recovered from the withheld queries, plus one
predeclared condition that no learner obeying the task could have met".

The Owner decision is narrow: does the four-chamber system count as recovered when
three chambers are generated and the fourth is the chamber the task handed over as
evidence?
