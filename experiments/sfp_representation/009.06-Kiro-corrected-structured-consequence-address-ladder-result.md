## One-paragraph result

The forcing failure in `009.02` was substantially an artifact of expressing
consequence as one of 84 opaque public Event identities. Holding the encoder, the
arms, the splits, the seeds and the training protocol fixed and changing **only
the output object**, held-out forced-third Event identity under the exact SFP code
rose from `0.0335` to **`0.7016`** on the primary LOHO family, a 21-fold move
against a `1/84` chance floor. The structural controls behave exactly as `009.05`
section 5.6 requires: exact code beats the certified non-automorphic scramble by
**`+0.5082`** (CI `[0.4583, 0.5595]`, sign-consistent across all 14 folds) and the
certified structure-preserving relabeling is statistically compatible with the
exact code (`D-B = +0.0506`, CI `[-0.0052, 0.1049]`, includes zero). Two findings
qualify it. First, the residual obstruction is no longer the `pp` forcing law,
which reaches `0.9762`, but the `PP` chamber field at `0.7158`. Second, a
nonlearned code-space tabulation reaches `1.0` under the exact code and **has no
solution at all** under the scramble, so the learner remains materially short of a
ceiling that the representation itself attains. The consequence relation is
substantially, not fully, constituted.

construction per `009.05` section 4.2. Three output slots, 17283 parameters,

**Rung 1 succeeds.** The exact code acquires the local forced-third relation

### Rung 1 is not dispositive, and this is reported as required

`009.05` section 4.3 asks for any exposed deterministic rule to be reported and
the rung downgraded accordingly. There is one, and it is decisive about what the
rung can carry.

The Rung-1 label is a function of the unordered input port pair `{pp1, pp2}`,
which under the exact code takes exactly **three** values,
`{(01,10), (01,11), (10,11)}`, each determining `pp3` uniquely. A three-row lookup
table fitted on training habitats therefore reaches **1.0** on held-out habitats.
At a quotient this small there is no observable difference between inferring the
law and tabulating it. The rung confirms acquisition; it cannot discriminate,
because a learner cannot exceed a ceiling that is already perfect.

### The `S3` symmetry, and why `B > C` here is not banked

`009.05` section 4.4 is correct and this result honours it. The nonzero `pp` set
has size 3 and `GL(2,2) ≅ S3` acts as all six permutations, so "the third of two
distinct values" is invariant under every relabeling of the local quotient.

Observed `B - C = +0.6682`, CI `[0.6429, 0.6927]`, 112 paired cells. **This is
recorded and takes no part in the disposition.** It cannot support `H_struct` and
its absence could not have refuted it.

The measured gap is also not the phenomenon `009.04` had in mind. Arm C's
perturbation is a per-habitat **edge** permutation, not a `pp` relabeling, and a
non-automorphic edge permutation can carry two Events that share a block onto two
that do not. That is visible directly in the frozen codes: the exact and relabeled
codes satisfy `pp3 = pp1 XOR pp2` on all 336 admitted pairs, the scramble on only
**156**, and the scramble's input ports are not even always distinct — its
unordered port multiset includes `(01,01)`, `(10,10)` and `(11,11)`, which the
local law's precondition excludes. So C is not a symmetry-respecting relabeling
that the quotient renders harmless; it is a stronger perturbation the quotient
cannot license a conclusion about. Either way, no structural claim is drawn here.

### 5.4 Fieldwise diagnosis: the obstruction moved

Within the repair block the bottleneck is **not** the forcing law:

```text
pp   the field the relation computes by XOR        0.9762
PP   the field that names the shared block         0.7158
```

`pp` is nearly solved. `PP` — identifying which of the four chambers the
consequence is incident to, i.e. locating the shared block of the input pair — is
what costs the remaining accuracy. Exact-address correctness is the conjunction of
the two, and `0.9762 × 0.7158 = 0.6987` against an observed `0.7016`: the two
fields are very nearly independent, and the address metric is essentially the `PP`
metric. That is a sharper localization than `009.02` could produce, and it names
the next obstruction precisely.

generalize; the scramble and the native coordinates memorize.

third. It is reported (`B 0.8511`, `C 0.7890`, `D 0.8476`, `A 0.5623`) and used
for nothing. Note `C_scrambled` reaches `0.8649` admission on LOFPO while scoring
`0.1771` on the address — precisely the trap.

## 7. Rung 3 — fixed exact address-to-Event resolution

```text
runs checked                                         672
maximum resolution drop                              0.0
unresolved predictions                                 0
address accuracy == resolved accuracy in every run   true
```

Resolution costs nothing and refuses nothing, and `ladder_resolver` proves why
rather than observing it luckily. The SFP alphabet is **exactly saturated**:

```text
2 sign values  x  7 Fano points  x  6 K4 edges  =  84  =  |Events|
```

So every syntactically well-formed four-field address denotes a certified Event,
and the heads cannot emit anything else — they are 2, 7, 4 and 3 slots wide, and
`pp = 00` has no slot at all. Resolution is total and injective on addresses,
hence resolved Event-identity accuracy is **identically** equal to exact
structured-address accuracy. Rung 3 could not have shown a codec drop, and any
difference would have been an implementation bug rather than a finding. The two
numbers are reported side by side anyway, per `009.05` section 6.

That saturation is why a wrong address is punished at full severity rather than
softened. `verify_no_repair` perturbs every field of every certified target:

```text
single-field corruptions probed                     4032
silently repaired                                      0
resolved to a DIFFERENT certified Event             3696
resolved to the other presentation of the same       336
```

The 336 are exactly one per admitted pair and are the documented codec symmetry —
both presentations name the same Event — not a resolver rescue. Every other
corruption lands on a different certified Event.

`UNRESOLVED` (`-2`) is kept distinct from `BOTTOM` (`-1`). A failure to denote is
not a decision not to admit, and conflating them would let a malformed address be
scored as a correct non-admission.

## 8. Deterministic baselines and the shortcut search

`009.05` section 8 requires the precise learner-visible features to be recorded
and searched for cheap deterministic shortcuts at every rung. **This search is
new to Issue 009.** `009.02`'s `FEATURE_ATOMS` are all *native* —
`same_habitat`, `sign_pair`, `block_id_equality` and so on — because the question
there was whether a native shortcut could explain an SFP result. The ladder's
learner sees the SFP code, so the search has to run over **code-space** features,
and none had been run.

12 atoms, 298 subsets up to size 3, fitted on each fold's training split only,
unseen keys abstain and are scored incorrect.

### Two ceilings, and the distinction is principled

The first search run exposed that `endpoint_pair` alone reaches `0.994`. That is
not a cheap shortcut. `endpoint_pair` is the **complete K4 incidence data** of the
input pair, and Outcome `021.05`'s Event-level law is *defined* on exactly that
data — admitted iff the endpoint sets meet in one vertex, forced third = the edge
on the unshared vertices — which is what `sfp.ExactSfpCircuit.admit_event` and
`forced_third_event` compute from and nothing else.

So the classification is stated from the certified relation's own definition
rather than fitted to observed accuracy: a subset is relation-equivalent when it
contains `pp_xor`, `shared_endpoint` or `endpoint_pair`, or contains
`endpoints_a` and `endpoints_b` together. This was written after the first run
exposed the issue, which is precisely the case `009.05` section 4.3 anticipates,
and it is recorded as `COST_CLASSIFICATION_RULE` rather than quietly applied.

```text
LOHO Rung 2               relation-determining ceiling   strictly-cheaper ceiling
B_sfp                                             1.000                     0.500
D_relabeled                                       1.000                     0.500
C_scrambled                        does not exist         0.232
```

### The finding that needs no learner

```text
relation-determining subsets, of 298 examined
B_sfp          14      smallest size 2
D_relabeled    14      smallest size 2
C_scrambled     0      none at any size up to 3
```

Under the exact code the certified consequence is a function of a **two-atom**
code-space key, determined with zero ambiguity over all 7056 ordered pairs. Under
the non-automorphic scramble **no subset up to size three determines it at all**.
`H_struct` is therefore supported at the level of deterministic tabulation, with
no learner involved and no training run required — the sharpest form the claim has
taken in this issue.

### Where the learner sits

```text
uniform-field chance                    0.0119   = 1/84 exactly
best strictly-cheaper rule (B)          0.5000
LEARNER, exact code (B)                 0.7016   CI [0.6585, 0.7433]
relation-determining tabulation (B)     1.0000
```

The learner clears the strictly-cheaper bar decisively and remains materially
short of the code-space oracle. Both bars are reported for every arm and family so
neither can be quoted alone.

Two further honest notes. Uniform-field chance is `1/84` **exactly** — the
structured interface is not an easier lottery, it is the same lottery factorized.
And a *pure* tabulation scores `0.0` for every arm, because the address carries the
habitat fields and a lookup table can only emit an answer it has seen; that number
is reported so it is not mistaken for a meaningful negative baseline.

Against `009.05` section 10 this is class **C** on every structural requirement —
local forcing positive, `B > C`, `D` compatible with `B`, fixed resolution
preserving — while falling materially short of the ceiling the representation
itself attains. It is labelled `C_partial` rather than rounded up to C or down to
B, because both roundings would misreport it: C alone would imply the consequence
relation is constituted, and B alone would deny a `+0.5082` structural effect that
is sign-consistent across all 21 folds.

section 10 is explicit that `B ≈ C` at Rung 1 can never trigger E. The E test was
applied only to Rung-2 full-address metrics, where the six-edge and four-chamber
structure is present, and both controls passed there.

## 10. Is opaque-token discovery warranted?

**Yes, and it should be scoped narrowly.**

`009.05` section 9 deferred discovery until a materially positive
structured-consequence result existed. One now exists: `0.0335 → 0.7016` under an
unchanged encoder, unchanged splits and unchanged protocol, with the structural
controls behaving as required.

But the discriminating question has changed. Whether a *supplied* code can carry
the relation is no longer open — the ladder says it can, and the code-space
determining census says it can without any learner. So discovery should target
what is actually unresolved:

```text
target      PP and pp, the two fields the certified relation COMPUTES
hold fixed  S and FFF as supplied identity
judge       against the relation-determining ceiling reported here, 1.000,
            not against chance
```

Two residual obstructions should not be folded into a discovery experiment,
because neither is about consequence forcing:

1. **The `PP` chamber field**, at `0.7158`. Locating the shared block is now the
   binding constraint on the full address, and it is a cleaner target than
   discovery. It deserves its own turn first.
2. **Closed-set habitat re-identification.** Under the declared default protocol
   the learner cannot name an unseen habitat at all (`FFF` at `0.0320`). No
   discovery result should be read as addressing that until it is separately
   resolved.

A recommendation, not a decision: the sharper next experiment is `PP`
localization, and discovery is better run after it than before.
