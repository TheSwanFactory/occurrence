## 5. Failure decomposition and primary diagnosis

For **A_cur_freeze** test (cleanest recovered-geometry + learned π setting):

```text
1 - A  ≈  0.595
1 - H  ≈  0.543   # unreachable by either legal program at frozen denotations
H - A  ≈  0.052   # branch-selection shortfall on reachable targets
```

All three seeds have `1−H > H−A` (reachability larger). Joint phase 2 additionally **destroys** reachability (H↓).

### Primary diagnosis

**frozen-representation/program reachability is the larger observed deficit**

(Applies to the audited checkpoints — not a universal architectural limit.)

## 6. One next experiment

**Catalogue-recovery / representation lift under freeze, not another policy sweep.**

Because `H≈0.46` while true-ε* `H=1.0`, and `H−A` is only ~0.05 under freeze, further π tuning cannot recover the missing ~0.54. Next: supervised catalogue CE / ray-implied labels (or other denotation teachers) in phase 1 to raise hard idx match above ~10/16 and raise **H** under frozen ε, then re-measure this ceiling. Keep ε frozen in phase 2 (joint unlock empirically reduces H). Defer modular/OT-advantage controls (017.09) until recovered H approaches B.
