## 3. Ceiling decomposition (re-measure)

Under Rec_CE / Rec_ray / B frozen denotations: **H=1.0**, so `1−H=0`. Remaining failure is branch-selection / policy (`H−A≈0.08–0.09`), matching the 017.16 B picture.

Under Baseline_freeze: still `1−H≈0.54` ≫ `H−A≈0.05` (unchanged diagnosis for force-seq recovery).

---

## 4. Interpretation

1. **True-index CE (Rec_CE)** trivially recovers full catalogue (16/16) and H=1.0 under freeze — diagnostic ceiling for “if ε were known.”
2. **Train-only ray/program-implied votes (Rec_ray)** also recover **16/16** and **H=1.0** on this densest-16 / r=e4 task: P_seq inverse on train targets uniquely identifies true catalogue indices; CE then freezes a B-level geometry. **No true_idx leak.**
3. **Force-seq alone** (Baseline_freeze ~7–11/16; ForceSeq_long no better) remains stuck near H≈0.46 — longer force-seq is not a substitute for catalogue teachers.
4. Policy A under Rec_* ≈ B (≈0.91–0.92): once H is restored, residual is the familiar small branch-selection shortfall.

### Verdict

**YES — catalogue recovery raised H materially toward B** (ΔH≈0.54; full gap closed on seeds 0–2). Rec_ray shows the lift is available from **train program-implied supervision** without true catalogue labels.

Caveat: Rec_ray’s perfect recovery exploits finite-catalogue invertibility of P_seq on this configured task; it is not a claim of general continuous-locus or modular-grokking trainability.
