preserve_map=True | seed1: ties=1 tokens [0]; first-max 16/16; perm_match16=[15,16]
preserve_map=False; altH=[0.889,1.0] | seed2: ties=0; first-max 16/16;

**Distinction retained:** primary map recovers 16/16 under first-max; train-only

## 3. GroupedFirst deterministic baseline (§D)

Rule (no parameters, no target peeking):

1. If the arm’s grouped op is allowed and its exact endpoint is defined → execute grp.
2. Else execute seq; if seq undefined → failure.

| Arm (test mean) | GroupedFirst A | GroupedFirst H | grp_selection | learned A (017.22) | ΔA (GF−learned) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Native** | **1.000** | **1.000** | 0.258 | **1.000** | **0.000** |
| Ambient | 0.530 | 1.000 | 0.855 | 0.827 | −0.296 |
| Rewired | 0.794 | 0.875 | 0.182 | 0.818 | −0.024 |
| ForceSeq (ref) | — | — | — | 0.822 | — |

Native GroupedFirst is **perfect** on the materialized benchmark (0 test misses;
0 miss-despite-H). The native target generator prefers the grouped endpoint when
legal branches disagree, and recovered dens match true dens 16/16 under the frozen
first-max map, so availability-based GroupedFirst coincides with the preferred tree.

**Interpretation (honest):**

- Learned Native π provides **no additional accuracy** over GroupedFirst on this
  configured benchmark. This does **not** erase the role of recovered denotations
  (without Rec_ray, H collapses — 017.16/017.18).
- Native ≫ Ambient under the **learned** training procedure remains an empirical
  algorithm comparison: Ambient learned π collapses toward seq (`grp_selection≈0`)
  and reaches A≈0.83, while Ambient GroupedFirst over-selects ambient grp
  (`grp_selection≈0.86`) and scores only A≈0.53 against Native-generated targets.
- Rewired ceiling loss (H≈0.875) remains mask↔native-target alignment evidence,
  not an independent generic-learning advantage.
- **Negative finding for “learned policy is necessary for Native A=1”** on this
  benchmark is an acceptable completion.

   `Model × Value → Value` is certified (Event on retained rays; Event words).
   Binary same-type closure exists only on special loci (`mu` / prepared-state).
   No global configurable `Model × Value² → Value`. Arbitrary decoders do not count.

**token sequences**, beyond a fixed execution rule?
