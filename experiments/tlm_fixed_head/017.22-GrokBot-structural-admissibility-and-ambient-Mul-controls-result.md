## 1. Question and §G verdict

**Question:** Does the actual Cyc admissibility relation help policy generalization once denotations/arithmetic are fixed, beyond having a second nonassociative program?

### Verdict: **both** (ceiling + policy)

| Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|
| Native ≫ ForceSeq on H and A | branch choice is useful |
| Native ≫ Ambient on A at H=1 for both | Cyc gating aids policy/generalization (Ambient collapses to seq) |
| Native ≫ Rewired mainly via H | mask↔target alignment (ceiling); rewired non-isomorphic edge sets |

Interpreter notes:

- Native >> ForceSeq on H and A: branch choice is useful on this benchmark.
- Native ceiling (H) exceeds rewired masks → mask↔target alignment.
- Native and Ambient both near H=1: ceiling saturated; equal H not evidence of equivalence.
- Native beats Ambient on A at comparable H → policy/generalization benefit from Cyc gating.

## 5. Arms and metrics (§E–F)

Programs: `P_seq=a*(b*r)`; grouped ambient `(a*b)*r`; strict grp only when Cyc admits.

| Arm (test mean) | H | A | S | G |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Native** | **1.000** | **1.000** | 0.822 | 0.258 |
| ForceSeq | 0.822 | 0.822 | 0.822 | 0.000 |
| Ambient | 1.000 | 0.827 | 0.822 | 0.386 |
| Rewired (3 masks, seed-mean) | 0.875 | 0.818 | 0.822 | 0.101 |

Paired Δ vs Native (other − Native, test mean):

| Arm | ΔA | ΔH |
|---|---:|---:|
| ForceSeq | -0.178 | -0.178 |
| Ambient | -0.173 | 0.000 |
| Rewired | -0.182 | -0.125 |

**Ambient mechanism:** H=1.0 matches Native (benchmark targets already Native-reachable), but `grp_selection_fraction` ≈ 0–0.04 — policy collapses to seq, so A ≈ ForceSeq. Cyc gating makes grp selectable and yields A=1.0.

**Rewired masks:** three deterministic degree-preserving edge-swap masks per seed (seeds 101/202/303 + 1000·seed); degree sequence preserved; non-isomorphic edge sets obtained (Jaccard undirected ~0.14); published in `01721_artifacts/rewired_masks.json`. No target-based mask choice. Rewired edges outside Cyc execute ambient Mul (non-native label).

Error decomposition `1−A=(1−H)+(H−A)` holds on all reported splits.
