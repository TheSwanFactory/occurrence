## 1. Question

`008.01` section 3 asks for the smallest exposed-input signature, **preferably
beginning with `E^3 R`**, where

1. at least two strict legal program classes are jointly defined on a useful common domain;
2. exact native endpoints differ on a non-negligible set;
3. no deterministic rule over local branch availability reproduces the target.

The framing assumes the fix for `017.24` is *more tokens*. The measurement says
otherwise.

---

## 2. Method

**Program space.** `017.04`'s partial role-typed planar term calculus, written as a
grammar over the exposed-input word:

```text
E -> e | Cyc(E, E)
R -> r | Occ(E, R) | Sand(E, R)
```

Shape counts match closed forms: `C(2n,n)` programs for `E^n R` with all three
constructors (1, 2, 6, 20, 70, 252), and the Catalan numbers when `Sand` is
withheld (1, 1, 2, 5, 14, 42). The `E^2 R` Occ/Cyc subset is exactly 017's
`P_seq` / `P_grp`, so the comparison is direct.

`Sand` was float-only before this issue, so the full calculus had never been
scored. It is now on the exact path.

**Availability ceiling.** Condition 3 is settled by a bound rather than by
beating one hand-written rule. An availability rule is by definition a function of
the availability pattern alone, so its program choice is constant on each pattern
class. The best accuracy any such rule can reach is

```text
sum over patterns of max over programs of |{x in pattern : endpoint(p,x) = target(x)}|
divided by the number of inputs with a defined target
```

This simultaneously upper-bounds GroupedFirst, ForceSeq, the random legal
selector, and every other availability rule. `learned_headroom = 1 - ceiling` is
what a policy that knows the target could win.

**Target.** A latent bracketing grammar over the Event **sign bits**. Each basic
Event is `e_i + s e_{8+j}`; the grammar reads the sign bits and selects
`index = bits mod n_programs`. Sign bits are a structural property of the
certified design, split the Events 42/42, and are not availability. This satisfies
the `008.01` section 4 firewall by construction.

**Sweep.** Exhaustive, not sampled: all `84^2 = 7056` inputs at `E^2 R` and all
`84^3 = 592704` at `E^3 R`, each under both constructor sets. Roughly 25 CPU-minutes.

## 4. Finding

**The binding constraint is the constructor set, not the token count.**

With `Occ` and `Cyc` only, 93 % of `E^2 R` inputs and 88 % of `E^3 R` inputs admit
**exactly one legal program**. There is no choice to make. The availability
pattern therefore already determines the endpoint, and the ceiling confirms it:
0.973 and 0.936. GroupedFirst sits *exactly* on the `E^2 R` ceiling and within
0.0014 of the `E^3 R` ceiling, so it is already an optimal availability rule.

This is a stronger and more specific explanation of `017.24` section 3 than the one
banked there. `017.24` attributed Native GroupedFirst `A = 1.000` to a target
generator that preferred the grouped endpoint. That is true, but not the whole
reason: **no target construction whatsoever can defeat an availability rule on the
Occ/Cyc-only space**, because the joint domain is too thin. The `008.01` section 4
firewall is necessary but not sufficient; here it is satisfied by construction and
availability still nearly saturates.

Adding tokens does not repair it. Going from `E^2 R` to `E^3 R` moves the joint
domain from 4.1 % to 8.3 % and the headroom from 0.027 to 0.064 — still below any
reasonable materiality bar.

Admitting `Sand`, the third constructor `017.04` already certified, repairs it
immediately. At `E^2 R` the joint domain jumps to 98.6 %, all jointly defined
programs disagree, and the headroom is 0.733.

In `008.01` section 10's vocabulary, the 017 limitation was **strict program-space
sparsity**, and it was an artifact of scoring only two of the three certified
constructors.

## 4a. Reported problem with `008.01` — for the Owner to resolve

**The experimentalist is not amending `008.01`.** `008.01` is left exactly as
published. The following discrepancies are reported for Owner disposition.

### 4a.1 Section 3's premise does not hold

`008.01` section 3 reads:

> Find the smallest exposed-input signature, **preferably beginning with `E^3 R`**
> or the next necessary size, for which \[the three conditions hold]. If `E^3 R`
> cannot satisfy all three, **move minimally upward** and document why.

The instruction assumes the escape from `017.24` is along the **token axis**, and
that the remedy for failure is a *longer* signature. Neither holds:

- `E^2 R` already satisfies all three conditions once `Sand` is admitted, so the
  literal answer to "smallest" is **below** the suggested starting point.
- Moving upward does **not** help the constructor set that fails. Occ/Cyc-only
  goes from 0.027 headroom at `E^2 R` to 0.064 at `E^3 R`. Extrapolating along
  that axis is not a route to a qualifying task.
- The axis that decides the question is the **constructor set**, which section 3
  does not treat as a variable at all.

Owner decision needed: whether section 3 is amended, superseded, or left standing
with this result recorded against it.

### 4a.2 Section 2 lists a constructor that had never been scored

`008.01` section 2 says to "reuse the verified exact/Phi-correct arithmetic and
evaluator from Outcome 017" and lists all three constructors including
`Sand : (E,R) ⇀ R`.

`Sand` was not in the 017 exact evaluator. It existed only as a float helper in
`experiments/tlm_fixed_head/probe_futurator_program_space.py` and had never been
scored on the exact path. So section 2's "reuse without reopening" could not be
followed as written for one third of the calculus. Promoting it was a
prerequisite, not a reuse, and it turned out to be the decisive variable.

### 4a.3 Section 4's firewall is necessary but not sufficient

`008.01` section 4 requires the target relation to be specified independently of
the candidate policy and to not reduce to an availability rule. This probe's
target satisfies that firewall by construction, and on the Occ/Cyc-only space
availability rules **still** reach 0.973 and 0.936. Satisfying section 4 therefore
does not establish that a task escapes the `017.24` trap. The joint-domain share
has to be checked separately.

### 4a.4 Consequence for `017.24` section 3

`017.24` section 3 attributes Native GroupedFirst `A = 1.000` to a target
generator that prefers the grouped endpoint. That remains true and is not
withdrawn. It is **incomplete**: on the Occ/Cyc-only program space no target
generator of any shape could have produced a different verdict, because 93 % of
`E^2 R` inputs admit exactly one legal program. Outcome 017's closure is
unaffected and its negative result is strengthened rather than reversed.

Owner decision needed: whether the Outcome-side note is narrowed, and by whom,
given that Issue 017 is already closure-ready.

## 5. Answer to `008.01` section 3

| Constructor set | Smallest qualifying signature |
|---|---|
| `Occ`, `Cyc` | **none** through `E^3 R` |
| `Occ`, `Cyc`, `Sand` | **`E^2 R`** |

Formally the smallest qualifying signature is `E^2 R`. **`E^3 R` is still the right
place to run 008**, for reasons the criteria do not capture:

- 008 asks about *multi-token* composition. `E^2 R` is the 017 setting, and a
  positive result there would not answer the question that was posed.
- `E^3 R` gives 20 programs against 6, a 0.856 headroom against 0.733, 36
  availability patterns against 6, and up to 12 distinct endpoints inside one
  pattern against 6. More room to distinguish a learned policy from a lucky one.
- Held-out **combinations and bracketings** (`008.01` section 6) need enough
  bracketing motifs to hold one out. `E^2 R` has four Cyc-free bracketings;
  `E^3 R` has eight.

Recommend ladder A at `E^3 R` with all three constructors.

## 6. Caveats

- One retained context, `r = e4`, carried over from 017. Definedness rates depend
  on it; the qualitative Occ/Cyc-versus-Sand gap is large enough that it is
  unlikely to be an `r = e4` artifact, but this has not been swept over `r`.
- The sign-bit grammar is one admissible target, not the only one. The conclusion
  about Occ/Cyc rests on the **target-independent** joint-domain measurement
  (4.1 % / 8.3 %), not on the ceiling under this particular target.
- For Occ/Cyc at `E^3 R`, `bits mod 5` reaches programs 0–2 twice and 3–4 once.
  The exact pool is recorded in `target_program_pool`.
- `Sand` is degenerate on the `Cyc` domain: the Theory-065 edge identity
  `(e*r)*e = 2||e||² r` forces `Sand(e,r) = [r]` on all 336 admissible ordered
  pairs. It contributes only off that locus. Verified on all 336.
- Availability patterns here are the *global* set of defined programs. A rule with
  access to finer local branch information is still bounded by the ceiling, since
  any such rule is a function of the input, and the ceiling is computed per
  pattern class — but a *finer* partition than availability would admit a higher
  bound. That is no longer an availability rule.
